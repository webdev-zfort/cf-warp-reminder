Privacy Policy – WARP Daily Reminder

This extension does not collect, store, transmit or share personal data.

What it does: it requests https://www.cloudflare.com/cdn-cgi/trace to check whether your connection is routed through Cloudflare WARP. The response is read in memory and is not saved or sent anywhere.

What it stores locally in your browser: the date a connection was last detected and the time of the last reminder. This data never leaves your device.

The extension does not access your browsing history, page content, or account information, and it uses no analytics or third-party services.
